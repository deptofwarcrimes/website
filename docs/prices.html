Statistical Methodology & Data Integrity

Large animated cards show the price published for the selected month (not the period average). Range and standard deviation use the entire chosen time window. The visual cursor moves smoothly between monthly observations, but card prices always reflect real published monthly data. October 2025 is omitted for white bread, Grade A large eggs, and ground beef due to a temporary federal survey collection suspension; all statistical moments and percentage deltas use strictly published BLS observations without synthetic interpolation.
