The big lesson: popular doesn't always mean true. A network of automated accounts can make one person's message seem like a crowd's opinion. Real people may then help it spread. Before sharing something surprising, check whether a trustworthy source confirms it.

Educational simulation · All accounts, numbers, and events are fictional · No actual social platform is involved.
A botnet can involve controlled devices or automated accounts. This example focuses on coordinated fake social accounts.
