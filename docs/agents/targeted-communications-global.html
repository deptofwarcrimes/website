Statutory Framework: 22 U.S.C. § 611 & The Subcontractor Doctrine

FARA (enacted 1938) is a transparency statute, not a criminal prohibition. It mandates registration for anyone engaging in "political activities" at the direction of a foreign principal. This section examines how DOJ's National Security Division enforces FARA on firms operating through corporate intermediaries like Havas Media, preventing companies from hiding behind Lobbying Disclosure Act (LDA) exemptions.
