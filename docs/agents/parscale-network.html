Analyst safeguards
What requires verification
  • Check business records, FEC filings, SEC disclosures and DOJ FARA registration #7649 for dates, ownership and reported amounts.
  • Cross-check pDNS timestamps, DNS zones, mail headers, SSL certificates and redirect chains before linking infrastructure to an operator.
  • Distinguish a party's reported product capabilities from evidence of a specific customer integration or data transfer.
  • Do not infer unlawful conduct or coordinated intent solely from overlapping staff, vendors, hosts or technical signatures.
