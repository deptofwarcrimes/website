Operational Context & Background

Following late-2023 geopolitical narrative challenges, the Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MFA), under Chief of Staff Eran Shayovich, launched "Project 545"—a 545 million shekel ($150 million) program to overhaul international public diplomacy. Rather than expanding internal state channels, Project 545 outsourced strategic digital operations to American micro-contractors and major communications agencies like Havas Media. Genesis 21 Consulting LLC was incorporated in Delaware by Yitzhak Tzahi Daboush in May 2025 to execute strategic messaging, influencer outreach, and digital strategy.

6-Month Supplemental Statement Delinquency

Under 22 U.S.C. § 612(b), FARA registrants must file a comprehensive Supplemental Statement every six months detailing all activities, receipts, and disbursements.

Legal Exposure under FARA Jurisprudence:

  • Continuing Violation: Omission to file supplemental reports constitutes a continuing offense for as long as the failure persists (22 U.S.C. § 612).
  • DOJ Notice of Deficiency: Formal deficiency notices bar registrants from legally continuing agency activities until resolved.
  • Proposed Penalty Reforms: Legislative initiatives seek fines up to $10,000 per violation for untimely supplemental filings.
