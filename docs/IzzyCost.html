Assumptions & limitations

These figures illustrate possible fiscal relationships. They should not be presented as a verified ledger of transactions or as a claim that aid is directly offset by incoming tax revenue.

  • Receipts base$4.92T reference amount used solely as the calculator denominator.
  • Procurement75% modeling assumption applied to the total U.S. assistance input; actual eligible procurement varies by program and year.
  • AnnualizationPer-second counters are annual totals ÷ 31,536,000; no payments are monitored.
  • MOU split$3.3B FMF / $0.5B missile defense for the baseline reference.
