Evidentiary Guardrails

Four Methodological Caveats

1. Ephemeral Live Indexing

Multi-guest TikTok Live debates leave minimal permanent web footprints unless re-uploaded to YouTube, X, or Reddit; low link density does not equate to low in-app activity.

2. Handle Cycling & Bans

Recurring ~90-day ban cycles fracture TikTok histories across sequential handles (@thenewoldermillennial, @unreelnessthegreat), making X and YouTube more reliable longitudinal benchmarks.

3. Media-Kit vs. Audited Metrics

Redden's 1.0M TikTok claim stems from self-reported marketing copy (oldermillennialmedia.com) and pre-ban peaks, whereas tracked snapshots on @thenewoldermillennial show 291.6K alongside 310.1K X followers.

4. Unverified Travel Financing

While Redden's 2026 trips to Israel, Poland, and Michigan and his CUFI/Reawaken USA partnerships are documented, no public financial filings confirm who funded the Poland delegation or Michigan stops.
