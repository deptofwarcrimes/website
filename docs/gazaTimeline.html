Sources and editorial method

The timeline uses UN OCHA situation updates, UN investigations, WHO reporting, and contemporaneous coverage from AP, Reuters and other news organizations. Each expanded event card links to the underlying report. The chronology describes claims as claims where the record is disputed, and separates incidents in Gaza from the nearby events that precipitated them.
